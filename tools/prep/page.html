What the exam will look like

This is a reading of the evidence you have, not a leak of the paper. The tasks below are the ones to be ready for.

Assignment 2

Count regression

Pick a distribution for a count label, write $p(y|x;\theta)$ with an exp activation, derive the loss, then code and fit it. Steps 1–4 are pure exam material.

Assignment 1

Gaussians and GMM

Gaussian maximum likelihood (mean, covariance), assigning points to the nearest mean, Cholesky sampling, the score, and why more clusters overfit.

Midterm example

Mean and variance from $x$

Normal with $\mu=\theta_1^\top\phi$ and $\sigma^2=\exp(\theta_2^\top\phi)$. The sheet gives seven distributions and seven log and probability identities.

Everything else

Concept and short calculation

Regularization as a prior, bias and variance, shapes in NumPy, neural-network counting, gradient descent behaviour. Multiple choice or long answer.
